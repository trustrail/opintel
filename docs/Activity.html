What changed. A row is 42 pixels and says what the request did, not that it was a request: a join, a read, a count. Rows expand in place, because reading a record is a check rather than a destination. The outcome is the second thing on the row and the only coloured one, so refused and incomplete are findable without reading. The treatment marks say what the answer contained — one clear element and four withheld — which is the fact an auditor is looking for.
