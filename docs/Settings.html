What changed. A setting is one line when closed: its name, its value with unit, and whether it is the default, set, or needs setting. The paragraph of bounds, defaults and consequences moves behind the row, read only when someone is about to change it. A warning appears only where a value is genuinely dangerous, like a group size of one. Changes accumulate into one save bar stating exactly what will change, rather than a Save button at the top of each page. Engines becomes a list with registration behind a button and the one actionable finding leading, instead of a permanently expanded form above a card repeating every field.
